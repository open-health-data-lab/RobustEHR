# Phenotype concept sets, by category

The manuscript table. Every concept counted **exactly once**, across the 22
declared phenotypes. Generated — do not hand-edit:

```bash
python validation/phenotype/scripts/phenotype_set_audit.py     # -> validation/measurements/phenotype_set_audit.json
```

Method in [validation/methodology/17-phenotype-association-method.md](../methodology/17-phenotype-association-method.md).
**Bold** phenotypes belong to the `mipa` cohort.

Counts are the SHIPPED sets, after two vetting-gated layers and one identity
closure: the LLM-proposed facet is gated by the verdicts in
`phenotype_vetting.yaml` (876 of 1,204 proposals kept); the closure facet — the
one-hop hierarchy neighborhood generated by `validation/phenotype/scripts/phenotype_closure.py` — is
strict-gated on its own verdicts (994 of 13,846 candidates kept); and every
admitted medication ingredient carries its RxNorm brand and product CUIs at the
`identity` tier, because notes write "Lasix" and "furosemide 20 MG" and the
matcher grounds them to those CUIs. Identity variants dominate the medication
column by construction and are reported separately in the audit
(`by_provenance.identity`) so no headline is read against the wrong
denominator. The raw candidate surface, model verdicts included, is what
`validation/phenotype/scripts/phenotype_export_review.py` exports for clinician review.

## How to read it

**The columns sum to TOTAL** (the eight leaf categories plus `other`,
explained below — summing the eight named leaves alone comes up short by the
`other` column). The shipped category taxonomy is a *lattice*, not
a partition — `disorder` is the parent of `disease`, `symptom` and `finding`, and
`procedure` the parent of its two children — so its counts overlap and a row
would exceed its own total. That is right for targeting, where `cat=disorder`
should select all three children, and useless in a table a reader adds up. These
eight are the leaf categories, and at TUI level they are disjoint: `disease` is
the T047 family, `finding` is T033, `symptom` is T184, `lab_result` is T034.

**A concept matching more than one is assigned by this order**, which is
recorded because it is a choice:

```
disease > finding > symptom > medication > anatomy > procedure > lab_result > device
```

The order is very nearly inert. `contested` — concepts matching two or more of
the eight — is **2** across all 18,074, so almost nothing depends on the
tie-break. An assertion in the generator checks each row sums to its total.

**`lab_measurement` is not a column, and cannot be.** It is by construction the
union of T034 (the analyte result) and T059 (the procedure producing it),
because it deliberately spans both readings of a string like `Hgb-9.9` — UMLS
and the SNOMED challenge annotators disagree about which one that is, and both
are defensible. With `procedure` and `lab_result` both present it has no
territory of its own, so a column would report a structural zero as if it were a
measurement. The **lab u** column carries the T034-or-T059 total instead.

**`other`** is 51 concepts across the whole panel — organisms, non-drug
chemicals, qualifiers. **`device`** is empty: no phenotype's set reaches one.

## What the shape of the table says

**The medication column is dominated by the identity closure by design.**
12,921 of 18,074 union concepts are medications, most of them RxNorm brand and
product variants of a few hundred vetted ingredients (hypertension: 2,705
medication-facet concepts over 214 kept ingredients). This is the layer that makes
"Lasix" and "furosemide 20 MG" fall inside a heart-failure target; one verdict
on the ingredient covers the family, and the audit reports the `identity` tier
separately so a reader compares ingredients to ingredients.

`metastatic_cancer` is 1,109 diseases because every primary site has its own
metastatic concept — plus, since the closure, the generic "Neoplasm
Metastasis" the notes actually write (831 of 1,388 corpus notes).
`chest_pain` is 115 symptoms and 78 diseases, the only row where symptoms
dominate. `clostridioides_difficile` briefly fell to 45 after the proposed
vetting dropped its generic labs; the closure's identity variants of its kept
antibiotics returned it to 189, and the concept floor of 50 holds again with
no waiver — see `tests/test_phenotype_links.py`.

The `procedure` column runs 3–32 and is where vetting cut deepest (292 → 213
before closure additions): procedures are supplied by the knowledge-first
proposal step, and generic work-up — screening panels, routine imaging — is
exactly what the vetting criterion rejects ("a procedure ordered for most
hospitalized patients is a drop"). Before the proposal step existed the column
was **1 concept across all 22 phenotypes**, because UMLS has no
disease-to-investigation relation to traverse.

**The closure facet is small on purpose**: 994 concepts across the panel
(7.2% of 13,846 mechanically generated candidates survived the
targeting-evidence vetting), between 6 and 104 per phenotype. These are the
documentation variants the evidence audit found the sets missing — "diastolic
heart failure" beside the hfpef seed, "Heart Failure, Systolic" for hfref,
generic metastasis, insulin above its analogues — each admitted by an explicit
verdict and pending clinician sign-off.
